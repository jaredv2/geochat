Welcome to GeoChat

GeoChat is a location-based discussion platform. Every point on the map is a conversation.

  • Click anywhere on the map to open or start a discussion at that spot
  • Search for any place in the world using the search bar
  • Login with Discord to post messages, vote, and earn badges
  • Discussions auto-delete when all messages are removed
COMMUNITY RULES

Be respectful. No spam, harassment, or illegal content. Admins can ban accounts that violate these rules.
